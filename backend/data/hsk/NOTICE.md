# HSK vocabulary data — sources & licences

`hsk30.json` is generated by `scripts/build_hsk30.py` for **levels 1–6** of the current official HSK syllabus
(*HSK 考试大纲*, published 2025-11, in force from 2026-07; the 7–9 advanced band is intentionally not included).

| Part | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| **Words, levels, readings, parts of speech** | Official syllabus, 中外语言交流合作中心 (CTI) — rows extracted and validated by `scripts/extract_hsk_syllabus.py` into `syllabus_vocab_l1-6.json` (all 11,000 rows were numbered and checked against the syllabus totals) | Public standard document; factual word list, attribution given |
| English meanings, traditional forms, frequency rank | [drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary) | MIT |
| English meanings for words missing above (≈150, mostly new in 2025) | [CC-CEDICT](https://cc-cedict.org/) (MDBG) | **CC BY-SA 4.0** — attribution required; derived data must stay under the same licence |

Hand-written meanings are marked `"definition_quality": "manual"` in the JSON.
Earlier versions of this app used the 2021 draft of HSK 3.0, whose level boundaries differ; do not mix the two.
