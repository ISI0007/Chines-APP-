# HSK 3.0 vocabulary data — sources & licences

`hsk30.json` is generated by `scripts/build_hsk30.py` (levels 1–6 of the official HSK 3.0 syllabus;
the 7–9 advanced band is intentionally not included).

| Part | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Official word list, levels, pinyin, POS, traditional forms | [ivankra/hsk30](https://github.com/ivankra/hsk30) (derived from the official syllabus, elkmovie/hsk30 and shawkynasr/HSK-official-Query-System) | MIT |
| English meanings, frequency rank | [drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary) | MIT |
| Dictionary definitions (original) | [CC-CEDICT](https://cc-cedict.org/) via the above | **CC BY-SA 4.0** — attribution required; derived data must stay under the same licence |

Hand-written meanings are marked `"definition_quality": "manual"` in the JSON.
